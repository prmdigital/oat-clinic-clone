For Pharmacies

OAT appointments, directly inside WinRx

Pharmacies are often the first healthcare touchpoint patients trust. Request telemedicine OAT appointments, collect signatures, enter urine sample details, and track requests — without faxes, phone tag, or paper referrals.

Pharmacy Guide   Contact Support
  1. Update WinRx to the latest version to unlock the OAT Telemedicine Appointment option.
  2. Book the appointment from the patient profile — choose Telemedicine or Life Tracking.
  3. Complete the OAT bundle — signatures by link, direct opening, or on-screen signing.
  4. Track & order — follow request status and order UDS cups from the same workflow.

Questions & Answers

Got questions? We've got answers.

Do I need a referral to start treatment?

No. You can contact OAT Clinic directly — by phone, callback request, or through a WinRx pharmacy — and be assessed the same day.

Can I receive care by telemedicine?

Yes, when clinically appropriate. You can connect with a doctor from home, from a pharmacy, or from another private and safe location.

Is my treatment information confidential?

Yes. Your care and personal information are kept private and handled respectfully, in line with health privacy requirements.

What's the difference between Methadone and Suboxone?

Both are evidence-based opioid agonist treatments. Our clinicians review your history and goals with you to determine which option fits best.

Can my pharmacy book the appointment for me?

Yes — pharmacies using the updated WinRx workflow can request an OAT telemedicine appointment for you directly, and you can keep your same dispensing pharmacy.

In crisis? If you are experiencing overdose symptoms, severe withdrawal, chest pain, trouble breathing, or an immediate mental health crisis, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.
