Who we are looking for

  • Physicians licensed, or eligible for licensure, with the CPSBC
  • Nurse practitioners with an interest in substance use care
  • Experience in addiction medicine is welcome, not required, we mentor
  • Comfortable with in-person, telemedicine, or a mix of both

New to opioid agonist treatment? Provincial preparation courses are short, and our physicians will walk you through your first weeks of prescribing.

How to reach us

  • Email info@oatclinic.ca with a short introduction
  • Attach a CV if you have one handy, a LinkedIn link also works
  • Tell us which clinics or telemedicine coverage interest you
  • Or simply call 604-670-6580 and ask for the medical director

We reply within two business days, and a first conversation is informal, usually fifteen minutes by phone or video.
