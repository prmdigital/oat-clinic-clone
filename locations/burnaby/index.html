OAT Clinic Burnaby

Our Burnaby clinic sits on Hastings Street in Burnaby Heights, close to the Vancouver boundary. It gives patients in North Burnaby, East Vancouver and the Tri Cities a quieter alternative to the Vancouver clinic.

Local questions

Visiting our Burnaby clinic

Can I transfer my care here from the Vancouver clinic?
Yes, and it is a common request. Because both clinics share one record system, a transfer usually needs nothing more than telling us at your next appointment. Your dose and your pharmacy do not have to change.
Is this location easier to reach than the Vancouver clinic?
For patients coming from North Burnaby, the Tri Cities or the eastern edge of Vancouver, usually yes. Hastings Street parking is easier here than it is downtown, and the clinic is generally quieter.
