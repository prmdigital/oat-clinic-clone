Local questions

Visiting our Surrey clinic

Is language support available?
Punjabi and Hindi speaking support can be arranged at the Surrey location. Please mention the language you prefer when you call so we can schedule accordingly. Interpretation in other languages can also be arranged.
Which communities does the Surrey clinic serve?
Most patients come from Newton, Whalley, Guildford, Cloverdale and North Delta. Patients from Langley and White Rock are also welcome.
